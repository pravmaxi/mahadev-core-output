      What this page does (and what it does not)

      • Downloads OHLC from yfinance, drops the still-forming candle, runs the WaveTrend detector and the entry/exit rules by calling backtest.py's own functions (detect_wt_events, _apply_filters, _qty_for, _target_px, _trailing_sl_price).
      • Entry = breakout level ± buffer plus slippage — never the live LTP, so it cannot chase. The moment the live price breaks a pending level (inside the running candle), the trade opens — you do not have to wait for the candle to close. This is the Scanner page's "Buy Triggered @ …" moment.
      • Logic check (banner under the run strip, badge under every symbol, and the Status column) re-checks the engine on every refresh: prices mixed up between symbols, a level / entry far from the LTP, target / stop / opposite level crossed while the position is still open, a broken pending level with no trade, stale or slow feeds, scans slower than the candle, a stuck loop, and positions on symbols you removed. A problem that does not clear by itself turns red.
      • WT Action column = the Scanner page's wording: Buy Pending (BO > level), Buy Triggered @ price, Sell Pending (BO < level), Buy Timed Out. Plus Trig Lvl, Valid (which closed candle gave the signal), Diff / Diff % (how far the live price is from the level), WT1 / WT2 and RSI.
      • Exits in backtest order: stop → trailing → target → opposite signal, with commission on both legs.
      • Paper only. No Zerodha call, no paper_trades.json, no Auto-Trader state. Its own files: data/paper_live_state.json and data/paper_live_trades.json.
      • Two honest differences from a backtest: exits are checked on the live price (not the bar's high/low, we have no ticks), and the fill happens on the first tick after the breakout candle closed.
      • Press 🧪 Verify vs backtest to replay the same candles through both engines and diff every trade. It should say 100% match.
      • Runs the "Simple (single position)" strategy mode only. Same-side averaging / alternate flipping, the martingale / incremental multiplier and the recovery-cycle overlay are not on this page yet — if your profitable backtest used them, tell me and I will add them before you run live.
